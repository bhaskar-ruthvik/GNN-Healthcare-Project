6 · What the graph adds: pre-registered tests

Learned message passing does not beat the same information as fixed summaries

Each row is one pre-registered comparison (fit, or insurer-compatible new picks at matched newness), averaged over 3 seed pairings, with a two-level bootstrap 95% interval (seeds and PCPs). Right of zero = the graph model is better.
