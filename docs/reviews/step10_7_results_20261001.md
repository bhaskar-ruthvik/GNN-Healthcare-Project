- **Chosen (validation rule): load penalty 0.2.** On 2019 its top-pick busiest share (24.5%) is below actual referrals (26.4%); its CI upper bound 26.3% just excludes the practice value, so "congestion reduced" is claimed under A10, **marginally**. Across all 5 recommended slots the reduction is clear (19.5% vs 26.4%). MIPS gain rises (+7.4 vs +6.6) and travel is unchanged; the cost is fit (44.2% vs 47.8%; PCP habit 47.5%).
- **Weekly capacity limits** cut concentration a little at no cost to quality or fit.
- **Retraining with load as an input** does nothing (the model already sees volume indirectly).
- **Retraining with busy doctors down-weighted** is a strong alternative: less concentration (29.0%), the same MIPS gain, better fit than the penalty (45.8%), shorter trips (8.7 mi), and the only configuration whose cautious gain excludes 0 on 2019 (+1.2 [0.3, 2.2]); it was not chosen because the rule minimises the busiest share.
Caveats: the busiest-10% group is defined on training-period volume; a top-1 list is each scorer's single best guess (history-based lists concentrate more than realised referrals); the fit budget was set after the Option A validation preview (A11, disclosed).
