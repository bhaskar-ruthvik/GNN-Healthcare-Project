# Step 10.5 — bias on the MIPS yardstick (2026-09-30)

Amendment A7 (exploratory, descriptive). Final configuration: v5 blend 0.3 (seeds 42, 1). One evaluation pass (validation → descriptive decision entry → test), then `scripts/step10_bias.py`. Groups with < 100 patients suppressed; reference = largest group. "Change in gap" = (group − reference) in recommendations minus (group − reference) in actual referrals. Outputs: `runs/analysis/step10_5/{split}_{group_table,gap_changes,masking_attribution}.csv`, `bias_{split}.png`.

## Findings
1. **Gender, race and age: no gap is widened or narrowed** in a way that holds on both years. Practice gaps are small (referred-MIPS differences ≤ 3.4 points between groups) and the recommendations keep them; every change-in-gap CI for MIPS and busiest-specialist share includes 0 on test (validation: one age-band conservative-MIPS narrowing, −1.8 [−3.4, −0.0], not replicated).
2. **Geography is where the recommender's benefit is uneven.** Patients in ZIP3 754 (already travelling ~20–22 mi in practice) get a smaller MIPS gain than the reference area on both years (change in gap −3.4 [−5.6, −1.0] validation, −4.2 [−7.4, −0.4] test) and, on test, longer recommended trips (+3.8 mi [1.3, 6.1]). ZIP3 760 shows a smaller gain on test (−4.1 [−6.8, −1.5]; validation −1.4, n.s.). Likely mechanism: the 25-mile cap leaves outer-area patients few high-MIPS options (see 10.4: looser caps raise the gain). Recommendations do move ZIP3 760 patients away from the busiest specialists (−0.20 top-1 share).
3. **Insurer:** AETNA patients' shorter trips in practice shrink in the recommendations (+1.2 / +1.1 mi change in gap, both years significant); CIGNA's MIPS advantage shrinks on validation (−2.8 [−4.4, −1.2]) but not significantly on test (−1.2).
4. **Removing gender and race (v4 pairs, two seeds):** 6 of 180 (validation) and 14 of 156 (test) group × quantity rows pass the attribution rule, all tiny (≤ 0.3 mi, ≤ 0.03 share, ≤ 1 MIPS point) and none replicate across the two years → **no meaningful effect of masking**, consistent with Step 9 and 10.2/10.3.

## Caveats
Exploratory: many strata, no multiplicity correction; read only effects that replicate across years. Race is computed/imputed in the source; correlated inputs remain in the masked model; area = patient ZIP3 (ZIP centroid distances). No causal claims.
