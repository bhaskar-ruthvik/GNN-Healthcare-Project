**Reading:** the recommended lists have clearly higher MIPS than the referred specialist on the point estimate, on both years, while staying as faithful as the PCP's habit and closer to the patient. The gain does **not** survive the pre-registered conservative bound (CI includes 0 on both years); that bound is dominated by counting no-score specialists as 0 (test decomposition: point +6.3 → spread-only +4.6, no-score-only +1.5).

## Findings
1. **The base model (no MIPS in the ranking) does not raise MIPS** (gain −0.5 test) but is the most faithful scorer (R@5 0.51 test vs PCP habit 0.48) and recommends closer specialists (median 8.8 mi vs 9.9).
2. **Blending the model with MIPS gives the best trade-off**: at matched fidelity it beats every PCP-habit point (test: blend 0.5 vs PCP blend 0.3 +5.8 conservative points [5.3, 6.3]). Model re-sorts beat PCP re-sorts but not PCP blends (validation), so the claim is "the model's blend dominates", not "the model dominates".
3. **Gains are not regression to the mean alone**: in every referred-MIPS band the blend beats the random row by +3 to +6 points (test).
4. **At the ceiling**: when the referred specialist scored 100 (35% of test referrals), the blend's top recommendation is also 100 in 74% and within 1 point in 77%.
5. **Load**: history-based top-1 lists (model, PCP habit) send 43–48% of top recommendations to the busiest 10%, above practice (26–30%), but across the full 5-slot lists they match practice (≈ 28–31%). This is the argmax effect of a single best guess, not a causal amplification. MIPS-leaning lists move load off the busiest specialists (test: model re-sort K = 10 top-1 26%), because the busiest specialists have lower MIPS (10.1).
6. **With vs without gender and race**: indistinguishable on every metric (test gain +6.25 vs +6.47; R@5 0.481 vs 0.478).
7. "Model − heuristic" at the §1 floor (+7.4 test) is structural (the floor is PCP habit's own R@5); the matched-fidelity comparisons in 2. are the informative ones.

## Caveats
MIPS is an allocation property, not proven clinical quality; single snapshot of unknown year; ceiling-bunched (1–2 point differences near 100 are within resolution). No-score specialists: professor treats them as new doctors, tenure does not indicate that; practice's own no-score referrals are excluded while recommended no-score slots count 0 in the conservative bound. Distances are ZIP-centroid based. Admissibility margins are thin (validation 0.462 vs 0.456). Secondary tables (excluded groups, ever-active, insurer set) deferred to 10.4.
